The Sound room

  • Record: tap the big red button, say something, tap again. Your film plays while you talk, so it lines up.
  • Make it silly: chipmunk, monster, robot, echo… tap one to hear your voice change. Me puts it back.
  • Loops are a little band in a mood. Beats: tap the squares to make your own. Play: tap the pads, or record a tune.
  • Sounds: boings, pops and kabooms. Tap to hear, drag onto the timeline.
  • Bring in an mp3 from your computer or phone.
  • On the timeline: drag a sound to move it. Drag its ends to trim it. Drag the little corners to fade it. Chop cuts it at the playhead. Loop makes it go round when you stretch it.
  • Keys: Space play, R record, S chop, Delete, Ctrl Z undo, , . step a frame, L loop.
